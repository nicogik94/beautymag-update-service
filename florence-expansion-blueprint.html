🗺️ 12-Month Omnichannel Master Strategy

Marketing alone won't win Florence. This broader operational and partnership rollout is required to bypass entrenched competitors (One Hour H&A) and solve the geographical distance constraint (the "Aynor Lag").

Phase 1

The Beachhead

Months 1-3 • Brand & Ops

  • ✓ Brand Protection: Register a "First Choice by Goude" DBA to prevent leads from leaking to the Manning competitor.
  • ✓ Local Operations: Hire 1-2 Florence-native lead technicians (Work-from-home dispatch) to guarantee 2-hour emergency response times.
  • ✓ Digital Wedge: Launch "Duke Energy Rebate" specific funnels to avoid bidding wars on highly competitive "AC Repair" PPC terms.
Phase 2

The Ground Game

Months 4-6 • B2B & Community

  • ✓ Builder Pipeline: Pitch Lennox bulk installations to D.R. Horton & Great Southern Homes (capitalizing on the 1,600+ new builds in the pipeline).
  • ✓ HOA Infiltration: Offer discounted "Neighborhood Maintenance Days" to premium HOAs like Forest Lake & Timrod Park.
  • ✓ IAQ Push: Launch free whole-home air quality tests specifically targeting the 9-month pollen allergy demographic.
Phase 3

The Flywheel

Months 7-12 • Scale & Dominate

  • ✓ Micro-Warehouse: Lease a small, secure parts/equipment locker in Florence to permanently eliminate the 60-mile "Aynor Lag" for parts runs.
  • ✓ Retention: Aggressively convert Phase 1's PPC break-fix customers into recurring annual Service Shield Members.
  • ✓ Referral Engine: Launch a "Neighbor-to-Neighbor" referral program leveraging your Lennox Centurion status.

Strategic Annex: The "Pre-Mortem"

Risk 1: Brand Confusion (The "Manning Leak") ▼

Risk 2: The "Aynor Lag" Overhead ▼

Dispatching from 60 miles away will destroy margins and response times. Must execute Phase 1 local hiring and Phase 3 micro-warehousing.

Risk 3: Digital Crushing by One Hour H&A ▼

Trying to out-spend the 8k+ review gorilla on basic Search Ads will fail. You must win via the B2B builder pipeline, community ground game, and Duke Energy specific funnels.
